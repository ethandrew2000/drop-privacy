Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the DROP mobile game. For questions or privacy requests, contact the DROP developer at ethancroft.developer@gmail.com.

Game progress stored on your device

DROP saves game progress and preferences locally, including scores, in-game Gold, upgrades, achievements, challenge progress, unlocked items, and sound or control settings. These records let the game remember your progress. The game does not require a user account and does not provide a developer-operated cloud save service.

Removing the app's local data removes these saved records. Device or operating-system backups may retain copies under your backup settings.

Advertising and consent

DROP integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display advertisements, including optional rewarded ads, and manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Ads involve processing by Google and, where applicable, its advertising partners.

Depending on platform, SDK configuration, permissions, and privacy choices, advertising services may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device or app identifiers, including advertising identifiers when permitted.
  • Ad views, video views, taps, and related app interactions.
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information.

This information supports ad delivery, advertising measurement, service operation, and detection of invalid traffic. Advertising services may use cookies, device storage, or similar technologies. Some advertising may be personalized when allowed by the applicable settings and consent.

Google describes its processing in its Privacy Policy and its explanation of how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your advertising choices

Where required and available, DROP displays Google's consent form. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the game's Settings screen displays AD PRIVACY OPTIONS, which lets you revisit the available choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers and applicable tracking permissions in your device settings. Changing or declining advertising choices does not delete locally saved game progress.

Children and parents

DROP is intended for a mixed audience that includes children, teens, and adults. The game does not ask players to create an account or submit their name, email address, photographs, or messages during gameplay. Local game records and advertising-related information are described above.

Parents or guardians can contact ethancroft.developer@gmail.com with questions about a child's information, or to request review, deletion, or a stop to further collection where applicable. Please do not send unnecessary information about a child. Advertising data held by Google is also subject to Google's policies and controls.

If you contact us

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you include are used to respond and resolve your request. Support messages may be retained as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

Sharing, retention, and international processing

Local game records remain in the game's device storage. Advertising data is processed by Google and its applicable partners under their policies, retention practices, and your settings. Support email is processed by the email service used to receive and respond to it. Service providers may process information outside your country.

We do not sell your support messages or locally stored game records. Advertising-related sharing and your available controls are described in Google's linked policies.

Privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, and to withdraw consent or complain to a data protection authority. Contact the developer for requests concerning information the developer holds. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google. Withdrawing consent applies going forward.

This policy page

This page uses no developer-added advertising, analytics scripts, or consent-gated embeds. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose operation may involve technical request information such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

This policy may be updated as DROP or its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
